Zaylaci summer camp

At a Zaylaci summer camp in Funen, we brought together around 60 children from across Denmark. They took part in learning activities, football, and time outdoors.

Working with young people is especially meaningful to me. I enjoy helping them take part in activities, meet new friends, and learn together.

Through these activities, I developed communication, patience, teamwork, and the ability to help children feel included in a group.
