Volunteering in Somalia

Working with Hasene

I travelled to Somalia twice, in 2023 and 2024, to volunteer with Hasene Danmark. I helped my team deliver and distribute supplies to people in the communities we visited.

I also translated from Somali into Turkish for my team so they could understand local conversations. Being able to communicate across languages helped the work move forward and made it easier to listen to people's needs.

These trips strengthened my translation, communication, and teamwork skills. I also learned to adapt quickly and take responsibility while working with people from different backgrounds.
